My Growth

This game represents my growth in computer science because when we started using sprites and Game Lab, I was very confused about everything and didn’t understand it, but by the end of this project, I felt like I really knew how to use everything correctly and efficiently. I was having a very hard time with the skill-building portions of our weekly assignments for unit 3, and they were taking me a long time. But towards the end of making this project, I felt that I was moving pretty fast in getting everything to work. I couldn’t grasp what each of the red sprite boxes did, but I figured it out finally. My only problem with this game is I wish I had added a screen of instructions before the game started, but it’s too late for that.
